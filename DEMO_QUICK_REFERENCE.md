**Voice widget not responding:**
- Say: "The AI voice tutor lets users ask any investing question conversationally"
- Move on quickly

**Charts not loading:**
- Say: "Real-time market data powered by our Railway backend"
- Continue to next feature

**Page load slow:**
- Say: "The platform uses edge computing for global low-latency"
- Keep talking while loading

**General rule:** Don't apologize, explain the feature conceptually and move forward!

3. **Explain WHY** - Not just what, but why it matters
4. **Use "we"** - "We built this because..."
5. **Tell stories** - "Imagine a student who..."
6. **Be enthusiastic** - Your passion shows!
7. **Smooth transitions** - "Now let me show you..."
