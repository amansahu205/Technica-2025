## 🎥 PRO TIPS FOR RECORDING

### Before Recording:
1. ✅ **Merge your PR** - Get all features deployed to Cloudflare Pages
2. ✅ **Test everything** - Click through the entire flow once
3. ✅ **Have test data ready:**
   - Login credentials: `test@example.com` / `password123`
   - Example news headlines for Insights
   - Know which lessons to click
4. ✅ **Clear browser cache** - Start fresh
5. ✅ **Set browser zoom to 100%**
6. ✅ **Close unnecessary tabs**
7. ✅ **Turn off notifications**

### Recording Settings:
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Frame rate:** 30fps minimum
- **Audio:** Clear microphone, minimal background noise
- **Screen:** Show full browser, or browser + small webcam overlay

### Editing Tips:
- Add text overlays for key features
- Speed up slow parts (navigation, page loads) to 1.5x
- Add smooth transitions between sections
- Background music (soft, non-distracting)
- Add captions for accessibility

## 🎤 SPEAKING TIPS

1. **Speak clearly and at moderate pace** - Not too fast, not too slow
2. **Be enthusiastic but natural** - Show excitement about your project
3. **Use "we" language** - "We built this to solve..."
4. **Explain WHY not just WHAT** - Why accessibility matters, why voice AI helps
5. **Tell a story** - "Imagine you're someone who wants to start investing..."
6. **Practice 2-3 times** before final recording
7. **Have notes nearby** - But don't read verbatim
8. **Smile!** - It shows in your voice even on screen-only recording
